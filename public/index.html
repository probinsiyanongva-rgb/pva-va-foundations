About this course

An orientation to VA work

VA Foundations is where a beginner with little or no knowledge of Virtual Assistant work starts.

The purpose of this course is to give the learner a clear, practical understanding of the VA world before moving into more specific skills.

This is an orientation course. It does not attempt to teach every VA tool, task, specialization, or job-search skill.

By the end of this course, you should understand:

  • what a VA is
  • what VA work can look like
  • what skills they may already have
  • what they need to start
  • what they may need to learn
  • what kinds of VA work exist
  • how skills develop
  • where to go next in the PVA Beginner VA Journey
The PVA Beginner VA Journey

You are in Stage 1: Explore

VA Foundations is the first stage. The later stages have their own courses and learning paths. You don't have to memorize this map; Lesson 8 walks through it with you.

  1. 1. EXPLOREYou are here
    Understand the VA world
  2. 2. BUILD YOUR FOUNDATION
    Become ready to learn and work
    Computer, internet, email, workspace, and other basic digital skills.
  3. 3. LEARN TO WORK
    Learn how work is actually done
    Communication, professionalism, instructions, and client work.
  4. 4. FIND YOUR DIRECTION
    Choose work worth learning
    Explore skills and possible VA directions.
  5. 5. PRACTICE & PROVE
    Build functional skill and evidence
    Practice realistic tasks and create proof of what you can do.
  6. 6. ENTER THE MARKET
    Present yourself and begin working
    Prepare your profile, apply for work, and start entering the market.
